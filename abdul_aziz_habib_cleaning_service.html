Our Story

A legacy of precision, trust, and immaculate spaces

From Humble Beginnings
to Industry Leaders

Founded in 2008 by Abdul Aziz Habib, our journey began with a single mission: to bring world-class cleaning standards to Ras Al Khaimah. What started as a small team of dedicated professionals has grown into one of the emirate's most trusted cleaning enterprises.

Over the past 15 years, we have served thousands of satisfied clients across Mina Al Arab, Al Hamra Village, Al Marjan Island, and beyond. Our portfolio spans luxury villas, commercial towers, retail spaces, and exhibition centers.

We believe that cleanliness is not just about appearance — it's about creating healthy, vibrant environments where people thrive. Every surface we touch, every corner we clean, reflects our unwavering commitment to excellence.
