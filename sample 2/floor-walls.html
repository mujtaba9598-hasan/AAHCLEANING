What we do

Hard surfaces, properly handled.

Marble & natural stone

Diamond honing to remove scratches and etching, multi-stage polishing to high-gloss or satin, then sealing. Also: crystallisation for high-traffic stone, edge and skirting detailing.

Ceramic & porcelain tiles

High-pressure machine scrubbing to lift ground-in dirt, plus grout-line deep cleaning and optional re-sealing or colour-sealing so it stays clean longer.

Other floors

Vinyl, terrazzo and concrete deep cleaned; epoxy and polished-concrete floors maintained. We assess the material first and pick the safe method.

Walls & ceilings

Washable paint, tiled walls, feature walls and ceilings spot-cleaned of scuffs, fingerprints, food splashes and water marks — kitchens, stairwells and high-traffic hallways especially.

How it works

Assess, protect, restore.

01

Assess & test

Identify the material and condition, do a small test patch, and agree the finish you want — gloss, satin or matt.

02

Protect & work

Mask off and protect skirting, furniture and adjacent areas; wet-grind / scrub with dust-controlled machines in stages.

03

Seal & clean up

Polish to the agreed finish, seal where appropriate, remove all dust and slurry, and hand the space back ready to use.
