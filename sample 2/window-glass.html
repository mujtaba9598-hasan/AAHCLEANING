What we clean

Interior, exterior & everything glassy.

  • Interior windows, both sides where accessible
  • Ground & low-rise façade glass (water-fed pole)
  • Glass balustrades, balcony & stair railings
  • Mirrors, glass partitions & office screens
  • Shower screens — limescale removed
  • Frames, sills, rubber seals & sliding tracks
  • Shopfronts & display cases
  • Glass table tops & furniture

Tall-tower rope-access work isn't something we do — but we'll happily recommend a specialist if that's what your building needs.

How it works

Wash, finish, detail.

01

Wash

Loosen and lift dust, sand, bird mess and water spots with the right solution and a microfibre/scrubber pass.

02

Finish

Squeegee finish indoors; purified water on exterior glass so it dries spot-free with no streaking.

03

Detail

Wipe frames, seals and tracks, polish handles and edges, and check every pane in the right light before we leave.
