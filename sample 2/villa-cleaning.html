Room by room

What "deep" actually means.

Kitchen

  • • Inside & outside all cabinets and drawers
  • • Oven, hob, extractor hood & filters degreased
  • • Fridge interior (on request), microwave
  • • Sink, taps, tiles, splashback & grout
  • • Countertops sanitised, floor scrubbed

Bathrooms

  • • Limescale removed from glass, taps & fittings
  • • Toilet, sink, bath & shower deep-sanitised
  • • Tiles & grout scrubbed and brightened
  • • Mirrors & chrome polished streak-free
  • • Extractor, cabinets & floor

Bedrooms & living areas

  • • Dusting top-to-bottom incl. high surfaces
  • • Skirting boards, door frames & switch plates
  • • Inside wardrobes (on request)
  • • Windows, sills & tracks; mirrors
  • • Floors vacuumed & mopped / marble polished

Throughout

  • • AC vents & grilles wiped down
  • • Light fittings, fans & spot-clean of walls
  • • Staircases, railings & balconies
  • • Bin areas & utility rooms
  • • Optional add-ons: sofa, carpet, curtains, façade glass
