Our story

From one van to RAK's go-to deep-cleaning crew.

Abdul Aziz Habib Cleaning Service was built around a frustration plenty of residents share — booking a "deep clean" and getting a quick wipe-down. We do the opposite: the skirting boards, the AC vents, the window tracks, the grout, the inside of the kitchen cabinets. The parts most people skip are exactly where we start.

Today the team covers the full range — fabric and carpet revival, villa handover cleans, marble and floor polishing, glass and façade washing, and the heavy post-construction work after a renovation. Same standard whether it's a one-bedroom apartment in Julfar or a villa in Al Hamra.

We're proudly local. We know the buildings, the communities and what a RAK summer does to a glass façade — and we plan the job around it.
