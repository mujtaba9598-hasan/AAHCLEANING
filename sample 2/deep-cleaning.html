What's included

Every trace of the build, gone.

  • Fine dust off all surfaces — incl. ceilings, ledges, beams
  • Inside & outside all cabinets, wardrobes & drawers
  • Paint spots, splashes & overspray removed
  • Sticker, label & adhesive residue off glass, fittings & appliances
  • Grout, silicone & cement haze cleaned off tiles & floors
  • AC vents, window tracks, door frames & skirting
  • Full deep clean of kitchen, bathrooms, glass & floors
  • Leftover debris & packaging cleared (bulk waste arranged separately)

How it works

Sometimes one clean, sometimes two.

01

Site visit & plan

We look at the size, the dust level and the handover deadline, and recommend a single thorough clean or a rough-then-final two-stage approach.

02

Dust-down, top to bottom

Masked-up crew, HEPA vacuums and the right scrapers — ceilings and high surfaces first, then walls, joinery, glass, and finally floors.

03

Detail & final clean

Residue removal, grout haze, full kitchen & bathroom deep clean, polished glass — then a walk-through so it's ready for handover or move-in.

FAQ

Post-construction cleaning, answered.

Fine dust off every surface (ceilings, ledges, inside cabinets included), paint spots and overspray, sticker and adhesive residue, grout/silicone/cement haze on tiles, plus a full deep clean of kitchen, bathrooms, glass and floors. Small debris cleared; bulk waste disposal arranged separately.
Larger or dustier sites do best with a rough clean after major works and a final clean before handover. Smaller renovations usually need just one thorough pass — we'll advise after the site visit.
Small leftover debris and packaging, yes — as part of the clean. Skip-loads of construction waste are a separate disposal job, which we can coordinate for you.
Usually within a day or two, sooner for urgent handovers. Fresh dust is easier to control than dust that's settled for weeks, so booking early actually makes the job better.
